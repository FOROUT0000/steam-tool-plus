去程序的【工具】->【免steam补丁】进行配置即可实现免steam游玩（Alt+Tab键可退出游戏全屏）
详细教程（游戏本体默认安装在D:\SteamGame\Resident Evil Village，不是请对应修改）

游戏文件夹 ：
D:\SteamGame\Resident Evil Village
游戏主程序：
D:\SteamGame\Resident Evil Village\re8.exe

Steam AppID ：
1196590

1.点击开始脱壳
2.点击应用基础配置

高级功能配置->DLC配置 取消解锁全部DLC
DLC列表  输入：
1731081 = Resident Evil Village - Street Wolf Outfit
1731080 = Resident Evil Village - Winters' Expansion
1456363 = Resident Evil Village - Survival Resources Pack
1456362 = Resident Evil Village – Mr. Raccoon Weapon Charm
1456361 = Resident Evil Village - Extra Content Shop All Access Voucher
1456360 = Resident Evil Village - Trauma Pack
